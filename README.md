Write a short, funny rant about an everyday annoyance — a broken printer, a slow bus, a Monday morning — and let a furious judge decide which one lands hardest. Entries are capped at 400 characters, so the punch has to come fast. Entry is free.

This entry's rant is about the supermarket self-checkout: **"The self-checkout screamed 'UNEXPECTED ITEM IN THE BAGGING AREA' at a bag of chips I hadn't even touched. Then it froze, summoned a teenager with a keycard, and left me standing there like a shoplifter while the queue behind me conducted a full citizen's arrest with their eyes. I came in for milk. I left a suspect."** (316 characters.) A specific, shared humiliation — falsely accused, frozen, judged by the queue — told in three beats with a punchline that flips the shopper into a suspect.

## Run it

